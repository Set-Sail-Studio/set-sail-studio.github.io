The essentials

  • We don't ask for your name, email or phone number. You play with an anonymous account.
  • We keep what you do in the game: your runs, your streak, your achievements and, if you choose them, your nickname and emblem.
  • Ads are served by Google, and they are not personalised.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • You can delete everything from the game, in Settings, with «Delete my data».

Who is responsible

The Last Tale is a game by Set Sail Studio, the trading name of Lizet Fernanda Amagua (España).

For any question about your data, write to us at setsailstudio.deadwrong@gmail.com.

We don't keep your location, contacts, photos or anything on your phone that isn't part of the game.

Like any server, ours technically logs the internet address (IP) of each connection, for security. Our provider keeps those logs for a few days.

What stays on your phone

Some things never leave your phone:

  • Your year of birth, asked when you first come in.
  • Language, text size, sound, vibration and the night reminder.
  • The draft of the story you are writing, until you send it.
  • Reading aloud, which uses your phone's own text-to-speech.

When you share an image of your ending, you send it yourself to the app you choose.

Who sees what

Other players see your nickname and emblem, and only if you have set them:

  • On the leaderboard: your streaks, survivals and rare endings. Without a nickname, you don't appear.
  • At your tables: how you did today, but only once they have finished their own run. Also your bets and reactions.
  • On published stories you wrote: your nickname as the author.

Our providers process data only to give us their service:

  • Supabase, the game server, with data stored in the European Union.
  • Google, through Google Play (payments and subscription) and AdMob (ads).

We don't sell or rent your data. We would only hand it to an authority if the law requires us to.

Some of these providers are US companies. When data leaves the European Union, it is protected by the safeguards the law requires, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Ads

In shortAds give you extra lives. They are not personalised.

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. We always ask for non-personalised ads: they are not chosen based on your interests or history.

Even so, Google may use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and your IP to count ads, limit how often you see the same one and prevent fraud. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your permission before showing ads, using Google's consent form.

If your year of birth means you may be under 16, we only request ads suitable for teenagers and Google treats them as aimed at minors.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Payments

The subscription is paid through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

Google gives us a purchase code. The server checks it with Google and, if it is valid, records that you have the subscription and until when.

Why we may use your data

  • To give you the game you ask for (contract): runs, profile, tables, leaderboard, library and subscription.
  • Legitimate interest: preventing cheating, abuse and offensive nicknames, and keeping the server secure.
  • With your consent: ads where the law requires it, and publishing a story you send us.
  • Legal obligation: keeping what the law requires about payments.

Accounts that haven't opened the game in 12 months are deleted automatically, with two exceptions: if you have an active subscription, or if you are the author of a published story.

How to delete your data

In the game, open Settings (the gear) and tap Delete my data. Everything of yours is deleted at once: runs, profile, streak, collection, nickname, tables, votes and stories sent. If you play again, you start as someone new.

Two warnings:

  • Deleting your data does not cancel the subscription. You cancel it in Google Play, under «Payments & subscriptions».
  • If a story of yours is already published, it stays in the game, but without your nickname.

If you no longer have the game installed, write to setsailstudio.deadwrong@gmail.com with your nickname and we will delete it. Without a nickname we can't tell which account was yours. In that case, it is deleted automatically after 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to see your data, correct it, delete it, take a copy, restrict its use or object to it. Write to setsailstudio.deadwrong@gmail.com and we will reply within one month at most.

If you live in the European Union and think we are not doing it right, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country. In Spain, that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Children

The stories are scary. You must be 13 or over to play: if you give us a more recent year of birth, we don't let you in. We don't ask for data that identifies anyone, children included.

If you are under 14, only play with permission from your mother, father or guardian. You must be 18 or over to send a story.

Security

Connections to the server are encrypted. What matters, such as the result of a run, is worked out by the server, not your phone. Access to the database is closed by default, and only what the game needs is opened.

Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will put the new date here. If the change is important, we will tell you inside the game.
