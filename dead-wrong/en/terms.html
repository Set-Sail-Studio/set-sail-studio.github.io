The essentials

  • Dead Wrong is a game of horror stories. All of it is fiction.

The game

There is a new story every day. You can die at any choice.

The stories are scary, with dark humour and no gory detail. They are meant for people aged 16 and over. Everything in them is made up: the places, the people and what happens to them.

Your account

You play with an anonymous account, tied to this phone. It has no password.

If you delete your data, or uninstall the game without being able to recover the account, you lose your progress. We can't give it back, because we don't know who you were. The subscription stays with your Google account: if you change phones and it doesn't come back on its own, write to us and we will help.

Accounts that haven't opened the game in 12 months are deleted, as the privacy policy explains.

Lives, ads and subscription

In shortYou can play everything for free. Paying removes the ads and the lives count.

Lives. You get one life each day, up to a maximum of three. Dying uses one. Watching an ad gets one back.

Lives, streaks, achievements and cards are not money. They can't be exchanged for anything outside the game, sold or moved to another account. We may change how they work if the game needs balancing.

«No ads» subscription. It is monthly and its price is shown in Google Play before you pay. It removes ads and means dying doesn't use lives.

  • It renews automatically every month until you cancel it.
  • You cancel it in Google Play, under «Payments & subscriptions». It stays active until the end of the month already paid.
  • Billing and refunds are handled by Google Play, under its rules.
  • Deleting your data in the game does not cancel it.

None of this limits the rights your country's consumer law gives you.
